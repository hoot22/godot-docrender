Hyphenation patterns distributed with Dockit

The binary files are unchanged from Chromium 142.0.7444.59, which redistributes
Android's hyphenation patterns in Minikin's HYB format.

Source: https://chromium.googlesource.com/chromium/src/+/142.0.7444.59/third_party/hyphenation-patterns/
Format: https://android.googlesource.com/platform/frameworks/minikin/+/refs/heads/main/doc/hyb_file_format.md
