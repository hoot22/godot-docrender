Salamanders are a type of amphibian, looking similar to lizards, with short legs and tails in both their juvenile and adult stages.

Rough-skinned salamanders use a deadly neurotoxin to protect themselves.

Several types of salamanders live in the temperate rainforests of the Pacific Northwest, including the Ensatina salamander, the Northwestern salamander, and the rough-skinned salamander. Most salamanders are active at night, preying on insects, worms, and other small creatures.
